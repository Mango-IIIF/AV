Transcript above the player

The same pieces, arranged by the page: the transcript first, then the player with captions in its stage slot. One controller keeps them in step.

<mango-av-transcript></mango-av-transcript>
<mango-av-player>
  <mango-av-captions slot="stage"></mango-av-captions>
</mango-av-player>

const controller = new AVPlayerController();
for (const element of [transcript, player, captions]) element.controller = controller;
await controller.load(manifest);
